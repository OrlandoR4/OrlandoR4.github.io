What We Do

In need of part fabrication for your capstone, research, or personal projects? At Space City Precision 3D we will take your designs and bring them to reality through high quality FDM 3D printing tailored to your specific needs. We offer a wide range of materials for the quickest of protoyping services deliverable on campus, and the highest caliber of consumer 3D printable materials for the most demanding engineering projects.
